Converter

The converter uses the first glyph (dashed frame). Clicking a glyph pins it instead (solid frame; click again to unpin). Colour shows direction: blue RTL, orange LTR, purple neutral (signs, digits). Dotted frame — a Claude proposal (newer scripts may not render on phones): ✓ accept, ✕ reject (it won't come back). The converter ignores proposals until accepted. Order = priority: drag cards or use ◀ ▶ (the number moves a card to the front). Drop a card on another letter to move it there — the old letter remembers it as rejected. A / i / W — checked on Android / iOS / WhatsApp (click: ? → ✓ → ✗). Edits are kept as a draft in this browser; “Download JSON” to replace mapping.json in the repo.
